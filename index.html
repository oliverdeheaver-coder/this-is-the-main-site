🗺️ World Exploring Guildford

Explore Stoke Park and Guildford town, collect rubber ducks, try every food and find all the Explorer points!

  • 🦆 Find all 20 rubber ducks (+10 coins each)
  • 🍔 Try 21 foods from 20 shops: stand at a counter and press E (F for a second food)
  • ⭐ Walk into the glowing rings for 22 Explorer points in 4 topics: Exercise, History, Nature, Fun & Arts. The new Merrow Dojo judo hall is an Exercise point!
  • 🎓 Spin up all 11 school caps
  • 🏃 Fun Run: on the lit red track, tap SPACE to sprint (watch your stamina). Press E at the start line for a 1, 2 or 5 km run and win coins
  • ⚡ Runs use energy. Food gives it back.

Controls: WASD or arrows move · drag the mouse to look · mouse wheel zooms · SPACE jumps · M map · T teleport · L Explorer list. On a phone: left stick to move, drag on the right to look.
